## Motion and effects

**VERIFIED:** The owner wants motion to feel synchronized and intentional, and has rejected laggy/poor-looking card transitions. A prior discussion considered scroll-reactive mood, blinds, accordion/parallax, and an infinite 3D carousel as separate demos, then requested undo and restoration of the Tubes cursor effect. Treat those as historical experiments; do not presume the four demos are current or accepted. The reference review says motion must express a state or relationship and survive mobile, keyboard and reduced motion.

**RECOMMENDED:** Keep the proposed RANGraph cover static first. If motion is later proposed, state one hypothesis, animate only the relevant artifact, keep project name/action stable, provide Pause and reduced-motion handling, and compare actual recordings on target sizes. Do not stack cursor field, scroll effects, card rotation and blinds on the same transition.

## Reference boundaries

**VERIFIED from `docs/REFERENCE-REVIEW.md`, `docs/REFERENCE-ENHANCEMENT-REVIEW.md`, and `docs/THREEUI-INTEGRATION.md`:** The owner supplied ThreeUI, portfolio, Astral Frontier, Samarth Patel, Codrops, FreeFrontend and other effect references. The high-impact lesson is restraint and connected storytelling, not recreating another portfolio or importing every effect.

## Acceptance bar

Before the first cover is considered ready for extension, the owner should see the actual integrated page at desktop and 762px. Validate truthful project representation, hierarchy, contrast, useful crop, responsive layout, normal scrolling, focus, touch, Pause/reduced motion where relevant, and loading/failure behavior. Separate three states in reports: implementation complete, technical checks pass, and owner visually accepts. The first two do not imply the third.
